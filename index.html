Australian Agriculture

Exploring agricultural production across Australia and how Australian agriculture has changed over time.

Australia's Agricultural Landscape

Australian agriculture generated almost $71 billion in gross value in 2020-21, but this economic output was not distributed evenly across the country.

New South Wales and Victoria were the two largest contributors, each generating more than $17 billion in agricultural value.

Hover over each state or territory to compare its gross agricultural production value.

Australia's Major Crops

Wheat dominates Australian crop production, reaching almost 32 million tonnes in 2020-21, more than double the production of barley. However, Australia's crop profile varies considerably between states.

Use the region selector to explore how the importance of wheat, barley, canola, oats and sorghum changes across Australia.

Livestock and Farming Industries

Livestock farming varies considerably across Australia, with different states specialising in cattle, sheep and pigs.
