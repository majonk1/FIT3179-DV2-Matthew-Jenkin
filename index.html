Australian Agriculture

From the Paddock to the Economy

Agriculture plays an important role in Australia's economy, it provides food and commodities for domestic and international markets. From wheat farming to livestock production, agriculture varies across Australia with different regions specialising in different agricultural industries and production patterns continuing to evolve as economic conditions change.

Australia's Agricultural Landscape

Where does Australia's agricultural value come from?

Australian agriculture generated almost $71 billion in gross value in 2020-21, but this economic output was not distributed evenly across the country.

New South Wales and Victoria were the two largest contributors as they both generated more than $17 billion in agricultural value.

Hover over each state or territory to compare its gross agricultural production value.

Does Higher Crop Production Mean Greater Agricultural Value?

Australia's agricultural production varies significantly between states, with crop production and economic value not always following the same pattern.

New South Wales produces almost 20 million tonnes of the five major crops, generating over $18 billion in total agricultural value. Whereas Victoria generates a similar agricultural value of $17.5 billion despite producing less than half as much of these crops.

Australia's Major Crops

Which crop does Australia produce most?

Wheat dominates Australian crop production reaching almost 32 million tonnes in 2020-21 which is more than double the production of barley. However Australia's crop profile varies considerably between states.

How Australian Agriculture Has Changed

How has Australian crop production changed over time?

Australian crop production has changed considerably since the 1980s with wheat is still Australia's most produced crop.

Canola production has expanded substantially over this period, while crops such as oats and sorghum have experienced fluctuations.

Use the interactive timeline beneath the chart to select a period and explore changes in production over time.

How has Australia's crop production mix changed?

While wheat has remained Australia's most produced crop the relative contribution of other crops has changed considerably over time.

Canola has become an increasingly important part of Australia's crop production, while the shares of barley, oats and sorghum have fluctuated.
